    Before you record

    Sidebar records the audio playing on your computer and your microphone. It never joins or appears in the meeting.

    • Get consent. Recording laws differ by place. Make sure the people on the call know they're being recorded and agree, where the law requires it.
    • Court hearings. Many courts prohibit or restrict recording proceedings, including virtual hearings, unless the court allows it. Check the court's rules or ask the court before you record.
    • Confidentiality. Audio is sent to Deepgram for transcription and the transcript to Anthropic for summaries. Make sure that fits your firm's policies.
